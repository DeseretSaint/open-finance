Open Finance

The finance app that lets you bring your own agent — and asks permission before it looks anywhere. Self-hosted, open source, MIT.

A SQLite file on your computer. Launcher opens localhost; installable as a PWA. We run nothing.

One question, three cards: same Wi-Fi, anywhere via Tailscale, or your own domain. Pair by QR — no env editing.

Phone

Solo on the go

Manual entry works without any bank connection. Plaid optional, everywhere.

Bring Your Own Agent

The feature you don't have to use

  • Read-only by default. Your agent starts with no reach beyond a summary of your accounts.
  • You control every read and write — including investments — and you can change it anytime.
  • It asks before it looks anywhere. Missing permission? The app asks you: Grant or Deny.

Works with MCP-capable agents: Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Desktop, Cursor, and custom scripts. No agent? Everything works without one.
